Safety guidelines

Most rentals are honest, but rental scams exist. These steps protect your money and your safety.

For renters

  • Always visit the property in person, or send someone you trust, before paying anything.
  • Never pay an advance, booking fee or deposit to someone you have not met or whose ownership you have not checked.
  • Ask to see ownership documents and the landlord's identity. Confirm they match.
  • Insist on a written rental agreement that states rent, deposit, notice period and rules.
  • Prefer payment methods that leave a record, and always ask for a receipt.
  • Meet in daylight, tell a friend where you are going, and avoid visiting alone when possible.

Warning signs

  • The rent is far below similar homes nearby.
  • The "landlord" is abroad or unavailable and asks you to pay first.
  • Pressure to decide today or pay immediately.
  • Photos that do not match the real place, or refusal to allow a visit.

For landlords

  • Verify a tenant's identity and references before handing over keys.
  • Use a written agreement and keep copies of all payments.
  • Post only genuine photos and accurate details.

Report a problem

If you see a suspicious listing or have been targeted, contact us. If you are in immediate danger or have been defrauded, contact your local police.
