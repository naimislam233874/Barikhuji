About Barikhuji

"Bari khuji" means searching for a home. We built Barikhuji to make that search clearer, safer and less stressful for people across Bangladesh.

What we do

We bring renters and landlords together on one platform, with clear rent and deposit information in taka, honest photos, and filters for what matters here: bachelor-friendly, student-friendly and family homes.

What we stand for

  • Transparency: rent, deposit and availability shown up front.
  • Trust: a Verified badge for landlords and listings that pass our checks.
  • Safety: plain guidance to help you avoid rental scams. See our Safety Guidelines.
  • Respect: fair treatment for every renter and every landlord.
